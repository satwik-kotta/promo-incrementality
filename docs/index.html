Where the units sold on deal came from

Every deal week is compared with what the same product would have sold at its regular price that week. The gap is the true lift. Part of that lift is taken from other products in the same category.

Why: the mailer matters more than the discount

Units rise with the discount and jump again when the deal is in the weekly mailer. Deeper cuts bring in more new demand, but they also hand a bigger discount to everyone who would have bought anyway.
